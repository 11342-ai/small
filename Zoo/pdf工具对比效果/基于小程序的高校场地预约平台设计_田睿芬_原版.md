     基于小程序的高校场地预约平台设计

     田睿芬*    ，程艳艳
     （郑州工业应用技术学院信息工程学院，郑州     451150）
                                         摘要：在万物互联的大背景下，微信小程序这个轻量级的应用在教育中的作用与日俱增，为教育行业带来新的发
 展机遇和可能性。为顺应互联网+发展趋势，设计开发一款基于微信小程序的高校场地预约平台，可实现预约教室、预约
 实验室、预约会议室，并一键通知上课学生补课信息、参会人员参会信息等功能，为高校教室提供实用便利的教室、实验
 室预约管理平台。基于小程序的教室预约平台设计使用了            MINA 微信小程序开发框架，通过 MySQL                    数据库管理数据。所
 开发的小程序能够满足预约教室、实验室的使用需求，避免了以往线下预约教室、实验室、会议室的繁琐操作。
     关键词：微信小程序；MINA框架；场地预约；MySQL

                         Design of a college venue reservation platform based on mini programs
                                              Tian Ruifen*, Cheng Yanyan
（School of Information Engineering, Zhengzhou Institute of Industrial Application Technology, Zhengzhou 451150, China）
       Abstract:   In the context of the Internet of Things, the role of WeChat mini programs as lightweight applications in education
    is increasing day by day, bringing new development opportunities and possibilities to the education industry. In order to   comply
    with the development trend of Internet plus, a university venue reservation platform based on WeChat applet is designed and devel⁃
    oped, which can realize the functions of classroom reservation, laboratory reservation, conference room reservation, and one click
    notification of make⁃up information of students in class, information of participants in the meeting, etc., providing a practical and
    convenient platform for classroom and laboratory reservation management for university teaching rooms. The design of a classroom
    reservation platform based on mini programs uses the               MINA WeChat mini program development framework and manages data
    through the MySQL database. The project ran stably and the developed mini program can meet the needs of classroom and labora⁃
    tory reservation, avoiding the tedious operation of offline booking of classrooms, laboratories, and conference rooms in the past.
       Keywords:  WeChat mini program; MINA framework; venue reservation; MySQL
